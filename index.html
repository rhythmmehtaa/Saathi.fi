Data Transparency 🔒

We securely fetch data via strict, RBI-governed Account Aggregator frameworks. This gives us read-only access (we cannot move your money) to your current month's transactions. This is strictly used to get a complete, accurate picture of your financial standing so the app can calibrate your budget.

Bank Not Yet Supported 🏦

Apologies, we do not currently support direct syncing for this bank via Account Aggregator. We have kept track of your requested bank and will notify you once onboarded! In the meantime, please try the SMS or Manual method.

SMS Transparency 🔒

Your privacy is absolute. All messages are parsed securely on your local device; your inbox is never uploaded to our servers. The app strictly filters and reads only transaction-related data from your specified bank. Personal messages, OTPs, and non-financial texts are completely ignored.

Welcome to Saathi.fi! We noticed you skipped setting up some of your Goals, Obligations, or Savings during onboarding. To get an accurate Safe-to-Spend calculation, please complete your profile from the Goals and Home tabs.
